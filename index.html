Course

We will first combine the two H 1s functions into ligand group orbitals (LGOs), then bring back the oxygen functions to construct the molecular orbitals.

Why keep track of the axes?

The labels pₓ, pᵧ and p𝓏 refer to directions fixed to the molecule. In this coordinate convention, pᵧ points perpendicular to the molecular plane; pₓ and p𝓏 lie in it.

Turning the eventual 3D view must not rename these functions.

Model · What are we leaving out?

This is the selected valence basis, not every atomic orbital. Oxygen’s core 1s function is outside this construction.

The geometry and atom sizes are schematic. No orbital shapes, MO isosurfaces or quantitative energy gaps are calculated in this layout. O 2s will remain symmetry-compatible; the course later neglects its interaction because of energy mismatch.
